Plain Maggi

A simple bowl of plain Maggi noodles made with just a few basic ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet Maggi noodles
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 Maggi tastemaker
  • Salt as per taste

Instructions

  1. Boil 1 cup of water in a pan.
  2. Add the Maggi noodles, tastemaker, and salt.
  3. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Serve the plain Maggi hot.
